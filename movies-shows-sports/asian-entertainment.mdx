## The Torrent Network (P2P)

</AsianSectionHeader>

<CinematicGuideBox title="The Pan-Asian Tracker Ecosystem">
  A crucial tip for navigating Asian P2P trackers: the ecosystem is highly cross-pollinated. Just because a tracker originates in Korea or China does not mean it won't host the latest Japanese drama releases or Thai BLs. If you can't find a specific release in one country's trackers, **search the others**. Also, please remember to properly bind your torrent client (like qBittorrent) to your VPN to avoid IP leaks, as outlined in our [Tools](/useful-tools) guide. *Additionally, many global trackers listed on our [General Entertainment](/movies-shows-sports/general-entertainment) page have dedicated Asian sub-categories.*
