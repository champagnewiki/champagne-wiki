## The Modern Meta: Stremio & Debrid

</TheaterSectionHeader>

Web streaming sites frequently buffer or suffer from dead links. For the ultimate cord-cutting experience, modern power users have migrated to the **"Debrid Ecosystem."**

<TheaterGuideBox title="How The Ultimate Setup Works" icon={<Server />}>
Instead of relying on unstable web servers, users install an application called **Stremio** and pair it with an add-on called **Torrentio**, which is linked to a paid **Real-Debrid** account (roughly $3/month). 
  
When you select a movie in Stremio, Torrentio finds the torrent file, but instead of you downloading it, Real-Debrid downloads the torrent securely on their massive, hyper-fast gigabit servers. It then streams the cached 4K HDR file directly to your television with zero buffering. Because Real-Debrid acts as a middleman, your ISP never sees you torrenting, effectively replacing the need for a VPN.
</TheaterGuideBox>

## Tracking & Media Servers

</TheaterSectionHeader>

With media scattered across endless streaming services, tracking what you watch is essential. Once you've tracked your media, use local software to organize it.

<TheaterRowDeck>
  <TheaterRowItem title="Trakt.tv" href="https://trakt.tv/" icon={<Compass />} badge="Tracking">
    The ultimate media tracking API. It integrates directly with apps like Stremio, Plex, and Kodi to automatically "scrobble" (track) episodes as you watch them, keeping your progress synced across every device.
  </TheaterRowItem>
  <TheaterRowItem title="Letterboxd" href="https://letterboxd.com/" icon={<Film />} badge="Discovery">
    A global social network for film lovers. Use it to log films, write reviews, and discover highly niche, user-curated movie lists that algorithm-driven platforms like Netflix hide.
  </TheaterRowItem>
  <TheaterRowItem title="Plex" href="https://www.plex.tv/" icon={<Server />} badge="Media Center">
    The gold standard for local media hosting. Download your own files, point Plex to the folder, and it will automatically generate a beautiful, Netflix-style interface that you can stream to any TV in your house.
  </TheaterRowItem>
  <TheaterRowItem title="Jellyfin" href="https://jellyfin.org/" icon={<Server />} badge="FOSS Media Center">
    The open-source alternative to Plex. It is entirely free, respects data privacy completely, and does not require server authentication to function locally.
  </TheaterRowItem>
