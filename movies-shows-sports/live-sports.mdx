## The Protocol: Acestream (P2P)

</SportsSectionHeader>

If you are frustrated by web streamers constantly buffering during major events, you need to transition to **Acestream**.

<div className="mb-6 font-body text-[14px] leading-relaxed text-[#4A5568] dark:text-[#8B949E] bg-[#FFFFFF]/90 dark:bg-[#141820]/90 backdrop-blur-xl border border-[#E2E4E9] dark:border-[#2A2F3A] p-6 rounded-md shadow-sm relative z-0">
  Acestream is a Peer-to-Peer (P2P) streaming protocol. Instead of everyone downloading the video from a central server (which crashes under heavy load), viewers share the stream with each other. This allows you to watch **uncompressed 1080p and 4K broadcasts at 50/60 FPS** with literally zero buffering. 
  <br/><br/>
  <span className="text-[#E32636] font-semibold">*Because it is P2P (like torrenting), you must use a VPN to hide your IP address.*</span>
</div>

<SportsSectionHeader icon={<MonitorPlay />} accent="zinc">

### How to use Acestream:

</SportsSectionHeader>

<div className="font-body text-[14px] leading-relaxed text-[#4A5568] dark:text-[#8B949E] relative z-0 mb-4 ml-4">
1. Download and install the [Acestream Client](https://acestream.org/).
2. When a major sporting event is happening, find an "Acestream ID" (a long string of letters and numbers). 
3. Open the Acestream Player, go to **Media > Open Acestream Content ID**, and paste the ID.
</div>
