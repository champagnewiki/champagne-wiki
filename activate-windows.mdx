## Deployment & Activation Guide

</ActivationSectionHeader>

Follow this standardized protocol to acquire genuine images and activate your system environments using open-source, community-verified scripts.

<Steps>
  <Step title={<span className="flex items-center text-xl"><DownloadCloud className="w-6 h-6 mr-2 text-amber-500" /> Step 1: Download Media</span>}>
    Acquire untouched, genuine installation images directly from verified repositories:
    
    *   **Windows OS:** Download [here](https://massgrave.dev/genuine-installation-media.html), [here](https://os.click/en) or [here](https://files.rg-adguard.net/version/f0bd8307-d897-ef77-dbd6-216fefbe94c5).
    *   **MS Office Products:** Download [here](https://massgrave.dev/genuine-installation-media.html) or [here](https://files.rg-adguard.net/version/5f2ad9c6-e111-76e8-06d1-56d44c136bae).
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span className="flex items-center text-xl"><TerminalSquare className="w-6 h-6 mr-2 text-rose-500" /> Step 2: System Imaging (Windows Only)</span>}>
    If you are performing a clean installation of Windows, you will need to flash the downloaded ISO file to a USB drive.
    
    *   Use [Rufus](https://rufus.ie/en/) to create your bootable installation media.
  </Step>

  <Step title={<span className="flex items-center text-xl"><KeyRound className="w-6 h-6 mr-2 text-orange-500" /> Step 3: Activation</span>}>
    Utilize the open-source Microsoft Activation Scripts (MAS) to permanently activate your software.
    
    *   Follow the documentation [here](https://github.com/massgravel/Microsoft-Activation-Scripts) to activate Windows and/or MS Office products securely.
  </Step>
</Steps>
