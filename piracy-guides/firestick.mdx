## Stremio & Trakt Setup

</StreamSectionHeading>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create A Stremio Account">
    1. Signup [here](https://www.stremio.com/register).
    2. Install the Stremio app on your PC, Mac, or smartphone.
       - *Note: This device will be used to install the necessary add-ons. It is much easier to configure on a computer before syncing to your TV.*
       
    <Callout title="Account Syncing" type="info">
      <div>Once finished, you may remove the app from your PC/phone, but don't forget to keep your Stremio login credentials handy. These will be needed to sync your configured add-ons to the Fire Stick.</div>
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect TRAKT (Optional)">
    1. Click [here](https://trakt.tv/auth/join) to signup for TRAKT.
    2. Click [here](https://www.stremio.com/acc-settings) to connect your account with Stremio.

    <Callout title="Is this necessary?" type="warn">
      <div>Linking your TRAKT account is not mandatory, as Stremio natively remembers your watch history and series progression. It is simply an added bonus. A paid version of Trakt is completely unnecessary for this to work.</div>
    </Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Installing on Fire TV

</StreamSectionHeading>

*(This method works seamlessly across Firestick Lite, Fire TV Stick 4K, and Fire TV Cube models.)*

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install Downloader">
    Install the "Downloader" app natively from the Fire Stick App Store (look for the orange icon with a download arrow 📥).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enable Developer Options">
    Allow the "Downloader" app to install third-party applications.
    - Go to **Settings > My Fire TV > Developer Options** and turn ON "Apps from Unknown Sources".
    - *(If you do not see Developer Options on a newer device, go to Settings > My Fire TV > About, select your Fire TV Stick, and click the select button 7 times to reveal it.)*
  </Step>

  <Step title="Download Stremio">
    Open the "Downloader" app, navigate to the URL bar, and enter this specific shortlink:
    
    `https://is.gd/dlstremio`

    <Callout title="Troubleshooting Links" type="info">
      <div className="flex flex-col gap-2">
        <span>• We use [is.gd](https://www.is.gd/index.php) to shorten download links for TV remote convenience.</span>
        <span>• If the link expires or an update is released, go [here](https://www.stremio.com/downloads), choose Android TV, and copy the **ARM APK** link. Then, run it through the shortener to create your own.</span>
        <span>• Alternatively, download the official [Fire TV App](https://www.amazon.com/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GGKS6F6YNYGHK6YG) on your smartphone to use it as a wireless keyboard, allowing you to easily copy and paste long URLs.</span>
      </div>
    </Callout>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Debrid Subscription

</StreamSectionHeading>

<Callout title="Highly Recommended" type="info">
  <div>A debrid provider is not strictly mandatory, but it is **strongly advised**. Without one, you will encounter buffering, broken links, and your public IP address will be revealed to the swarm.</div>
</Callout>

A debrid service is a premium subscription that lets you stream torrents securely, acting similarly to a seedbox. The essential distinction is that when you link a debrid service to Torrentio, the media files are securely cached on their high-speed servers. This allows for immediate, buffer-free streaming of 4K content without waiting for downloads or exposing your IP.
