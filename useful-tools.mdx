## System-Wide Adblocking 

</SectionHeader>

Blocking ads, trackers, and malicious domains at the network level is far more efficient than relying solely on browser extensions. It secures your entire device, including background apps.

### Android Filtering

<ToolDeck>
  <ToolCard title="RethinkDNS" href="https://rethinkdns.com/app" icon={<Smartphone />} badge="Highly Customizable" badgeStyle="indigo">
An open-source DNS + Firewall app. It allows you to select highly specific blocklists and route them via secure DoT (DNS-over-TLS).
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Mullvad DNS" href="https://mullvad.net/en/help/dns-over-https-and-dns-over-tls#how-to-use" icon={<Lock />} badge="Plug & Play" badgeStyle="emerald">
A zero-configuration, highly private DNS resolver that blocks ads and trackers natively. Perfect if you don't want to manage blocklists manually.
  </ToolCard>
</ToolDeck>

**Method 1: RethinkDNS Setup**
First, visit [rethinkdns.com/configure](https://www.rethinkdns.com/configure) and select the following recommended lists:
<FilterCloud 
  title="Recommended RethinkDNS Blocklists" 
  filters={[
    "NoCoin (Cryptojacking)", 
    "1HOSTS (EXTRA)", 
    "OISD (BIG)", 
    "ADS (The Block List Project)", 
    "GOODBYEADS HOSTS", 
    "ADS AND TRACKING (Lightswitch05)"
  ]} 
/>

Once selected, toggle the URL format to **DoT** and copy the generated URL. Then, configure your Android device:

<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Open the Android <strong>Settings</strong> app.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Navigate to <strong>Network & internet</strong>.</Step>
  <Step num="3">Tap on <strong>Private DNS</strong>.</Step>
  <Step num="4">Select <strong>Private DNS provider hostname</strong>.</Step>
  <Step num="5">Paste your copied RethinkDNS DoT URL and hit Save.</Step>
</StepList>

**Method 2: Mullvad DNS Setup**
If you prefer Mullvad, select the hostname that best fits your needs:
- <ConfigCode>base.dns.mullvad.net</ConfigCode> (Blocks Ads, Trackers, Malware)
- <ConfigCode>extended.dns.mullvad.net</ConfigCode> (Blocks Ads, Trackers, Malware, Social)
- <ConfigCode>family.dns.mullvad.net</ConfigCode> (Blocks Ads, Trackers, Malware, Adult, Gambling)
- <ConfigCode>all.dns.mullvad.net</ConfigCode> (Blocks everything above)

Apply the chosen hostname using the exact same Android Private DNS steps listed above.

*(Note: If you only want browser-level blocking on Android, you can use the free [Adguard for Android](https://adguard.com/en/adguard-android/overview.html). For the system-wide premium version, check out the resources on our [Mobile Page](/mobile).)*

### iOS & macOS Filtering

<ToolDeck>
  <ToolCard title="wBlock" href="https://github.com/0xCUB3/wBlock" icon={<Apple />} badge="Open Source" badgeStyle="neutral">
A phenomenal, completely free and open-source adblocker built strictly for the Safari browser across macOS and iOS. It operates on Apple's native declarative API for zero-overhead performance and fully supports custom filter lists, Greasemonkey userscripts, and iCloud sync.

Native Apple Configuration Profiles that force your entire device to route DNS queries through Mullvad's adblocking servers.
  </ToolCard>
</ToolDeck>

**Mullvad System-Wide DNS (iOS 18+)**
<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Go to the <a href="https://github.com/mullvad/encrypted-dns-profiles" target="_blank">Mullvad Profiles GitHub</a> via Safari.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Tap a profile category (e.g., <strong>base</strong> or <strong>extended</strong>) in DoH or DoT format.</Step>
  <Step num="3">Tap <strong>View raw</strong>, then <strong>Allow</strong> to download the profile.</Step>
  <Step num="4">Open the <strong>Files</strong> app, go to Recents, and tap the downloaded profile.</Step>
  <Step num="5">Open the <strong>Settings</strong> app. Tap the newly present <strong>Profile Downloaded</strong> at the top.</Step>
  <Step num="6">Tap <strong>Install</strong>, enter your passcode, and tap Install again. Tap <strong>Done</strong>.</Step>
</StepList>

**Mullvad System-Wide DNS (macOS 15+)**
*(For macOS 13 and 14, follow [these instructions](https://mullvad.net/en/help/dns-over-https-and-dns-over-tls#how-to-use).)*
<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Download your preferred profile from the <a href="https://github.com/mullvad/encrypted-dns-profiles" target="_blank">GitHub repository</a>.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Open the <strong>System Settings</strong> app.</Step>
  <Step num="3">Click on <strong>Profile Downloaded</strong> in the left column under your username.</Step>
  <Step num="4">Double-click the downloaded profile, click <strong>Install...</strong> and enter your password.</Step>
  <Step num="5">Manage profiles anytime via <strong>General > Device Management</strong>.</Step>
</StepList>

## Torrenting & Network Security

</SectionHeader>

When torrenting, ensuring your traffic doesn't leak outside your VPN tunnel is critical. **Always bind your torrent client directly to your VPN's network interface.**

<ToolDeck>
  <ToolCard title="qBittorrent" href="https://www.qbittorrent.org/" icon={<Network />} badge="Windows / Mac" badgeStyle="emerald">
A feature-rich, open-source client with an integrated search engine. It contains no ads and is universally respected.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Transmission" href="https://transmissionbt.com/" icon={<Apple />} badge="macOS / Linux" badgeStyle="neutral">
A beautifully lightweight, open-source client known for its extreme simplicity and low system resource footprint.

</ToolDeck>

### How to Bind Your VPN in qBittorrent

**For Windows:**
<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Start your VPN and connect to a server.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Launch qBittorrent and open <strong>Preferences</strong> (Tools > Options).</Step>
  <Step num="3">Navigate to the <strong>Advanced</strong> tab.</Step>
  <Step num="4">Find <strong>Network Interface</strong> and change it from 'Any Interface' to your VPN's adapter (e.g., Mullvad, Proton, WireGuard).</Step>
  <Step num="5">Restart qBittorrent. If the VPN disconnects, torrents will instantly stop routing.</Step>
</StepList>

**For macOS:**
<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Connect to your VPN.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Open the <strong>Terminal</strong> app (Applications > Utilities).</Step>
  <Step num="3">Execute the command: <ConfigCode>ifconfig | grep -A 2 utun</ConfigCode></Step>
  <Step num="4">Identify the `utun` interface showing an internal IP like `inet 10.x.x.x` (e.g., <strong>utun3</strong>).</Step>
  <Step num="5">Open qBittorrent <strong>Preferences</strong> > <strong>Advanced</strong> tab.</Step>
  <Step num="6">Set the Network Interface to match the identified utun interface (e.g., utun3).</Step>
  <Step num="7">Restart qBittorrent to apply.</Step>
</StepList>
