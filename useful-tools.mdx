## System-Wide Adblocking 

</SectionHeader>

Browser adblockers are still the most effective way to remove ads from websites, but they only protect the browser itself. DNS filtering adds a system-wide layer that can block known advertising, tracking, and malicious domains at the DNS level across apps, games, and background services. 

*DNS performance varies significantly by location and network; these recommendations prioritize filtering, privacy, reliability, and ease of use rather than claiming one resolver is universally the fastest. For a quick comparison, you can test providers from your own connection using tools like [DNS Speed Test](https://dnsspeedtest.online/) before switching permanently.*

### Android Filtering

  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Control D" href="https://controld.com/free-dns#quick-setups" icon={<Network />} badge="Flexible" badgeStyle="rose">
    Highly reliable free public DNS with ready-made Ads & Tracking, Malware, and Family filtering presets.
  </ToolCard>
</ToolDeck>

**Method 1: RethinkDNS Setup**
RethinkDNS is our preferred default for Android users who want to combine DNS filtering with firewall controls. Visit [rethinkdns.com/configure](https://www.rethinkdns.com/configure) and select the following recommended lists:

<FilterCloud 
  title="Recommended RethinkDNS Blocklists" 
  filters={[
    "NoCoin (Cryptojacking)",

    "ADS (The Block List Project)",

  ]} 
/>

Once selected, toggle the URL format to **DoT** and copy the generated URL. Then, configure your Android device:

<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Open the Android <strong>Settings</strong> app.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Navigate to <strong>Network & internet</strong> (or Connections).</Step>
  <Step num="3">Tap on <strong>Private DNS</strong>.</Step>
  <Step num="4">Select <strong>Private DNS provider hostname</strong>.</Step>
  <Step num="5">Paste your copied RethinkDNS DoT URL and hit Save.</Step>
</StepList>

**Method 2: Plug & Play DNS (AdGuard or Control D)**
If you prefer a simpler setup without configuring custom blocklists, you can use a public filtering endpoint to block known advertising and tracking domains:

<ConfigCode>dns.adguard-dns.com</ConfigCode> *(or <ConfigCode>p2.freedns.controld.com</ConfigCode>)*

Paste your chosen hostname into your Android Private DNS settings using the exact same steps listed in Method 1.

**Method 1: AdGuard DNS (Native Apple Profile)**
AdGuard provides official, direct-download configuration profiles that route your device's DNS queries through their ad-blocking servers at the OS level.

<StepList>

  <Step num="4"><strong>On macOS:</strong> Open <strong>System Settings</strong>. Click on <strong>Profile Downloaded</strong> in the left sidebar, double-click the profile, and click Install.</Step>

## Torrenting & Network Security

</SectionHeader>

When torrenting, ensuring your traffic doesn't leak outside your VPN tunnel is critical. **Always bind your torrent client directly to your VPN's network interface.**

<ToolDeck>
  <ToolCard title="qBittorrent" href="https://www.qbittorrent.org/" icon={<Network />} badge="Windows / Mac" badgeStyle="emerald">
A feature-rich, open-source client with an integrated search engine. It contains no ads and is universally respected.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Transmission" href="https://transmissionbt.com/" icon={<Apple />} badge="macOS / Linux" badgeStyle="neutral">
A beautifully lightweight, open-source client known for its extreme simplicity and low system resource footprint.
  </ToolCard>
</ToolDeck>

### How to Bind Your VPN in qBittorrent

**For Windows:**
<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Start your VPN and connect to a server.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Launch qBittorrent and open <strong>Preferences</strong> (Tools > Options).</Step>
  <Step num="3">Navigate to the <strong>Advanced</strong> tab.</Step>
  <Step num="4">Find <strong>Network Interface</strong> and change it from 'Any Interface' to your VPN's adapter (e.g., Mullvad, Proton, WireGuard).</Step>
  <Step num="5">Restart qBittorrent. If the VPN disconnects, torrents will instantly stop routing.</Step>
</StepList>

**For macOS:**
<StepList>
  <Step num="1">Connect to your VPN.</Step>
  <Step num="2">Open the <strong>Terminal</strong> app (Applications > Utilities).</Step>
  <Step num="3">Execute the command: <ConfigCode>ifconfig | grep -A 2 utun</ConfigCode></Step>
  <Step num="4">Identify the `utun` interface showing an internal IP like `inet 10.x.x.x` (e.g., <strong>utun3</strong>).</Step>
  <Step num="5">Open qBittorrent <strong>Preferences</strong> > <strong>Advanced</strong> tab.</Step>
  <Step num="6">Set the Network Interface to match the identified utun interface (e.g., utun3).</Step>
  <Step num="7">Restart qBittorrent to apply.</Step>
</StepList>
