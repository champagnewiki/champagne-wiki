<GuideBox title="A few more worth bookmarking" icon={<BookmarkPlus />} tone="neutral">

- [All Manga](https://allmanga.to/anime?tr=sub&cty=ALL) — wide catalog, syncs progress if you sign in

</GuideBox>

### Scrapers

</WeebSectionHeading>

Instead of hosting anything themselves, these sites stitch together feeds from several backend sources — so if one goes down, the show usually keeps playing from another.

<PillDeck>
  <WeebPill title="AniLight" href="https://anilight.live/" type="tool" />
  <WeebPill title="AnimeX" href="https://animex.one/home" type="tool" />
  <WeebPill title="Luna" href="https://luna-stream.me/" type="tool" />
</PillDeck>

<GuideBox title="What's actually powering each one" icon={<Network />} tone="neutral">
Useful to know if a specific episode is missing or lagging on one scraper — it might just be that backend having a bad day:
- **AniLight** draws from AnimePahe, Anibd, AniDB, AniNeko, Anikoto, and AllManga
- **AnimeX** pulls from AniDB, Anibd, AniNeko, AnimeOnsen, AllManga, and Animeverse
- **Luna** combines Anikoto, Aniwave, Anibd, AniDB, AnimeGG, AllManga, AniNeko, AnimeHeaven, and Animeverse
- **Miruro** aggregates AnimeDunya, AniDB, AnimeGG, AnimePahe, AniNeko, KickAssAnime, AllManga, and Anikoto
</GuideBox>

## Manga

</WeebSectionHeading>

<ZoneIntro accent="matcha">
Reader sites vary wildly in library size, upload speed, and how much clutter you have to fight through. Here's the shortlist worth your time.
</ZoneIntro>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<Library />} accent="matcha">

### Reading Online

</WeebSectionHeading>

<PillDeck>
  <WeebPill title="Kagane" href="https://kagane.to/" type="site" tagLabel="37k titles" />
  <WeebPill title="Weeb Central" href="https://weebcentral.com/" type="site" tagLabel="11k titles" />
  <WeebPill title="Comix" href="https://comix.to/" type="site" tagLabel="91k titles" />
  <WeebPill title="Atsumaru" href="https://atsu.moe/" type="site" tagLabel="18k titles" />
  <WeebPill title="MangaDotNet" href="https://mangadot.net/" type="site" tagLabel="32k titles" />
</PillDeck>

<GuideBox title="Bigger libraries, worth a look too" icon={<BookmarkPlus />} tone="neutral">
None of these missed the cut by much. Each has its own quirk that might suit you better depending on what you're reading:
- [OniSaga](https://onisaga.com/home) has one of the largest catalogs around, at roughly 76k titles
- [MangaBall](https://mangaball.net/) leads the pack in raw size with over 150k titles listed
- [Mangahub](https://mangahub.io/) and [MangaKatana](https://mangakatana.com/) both keep things simple with clean layouts
- [MangaFire](https://mangafire.to/) offers a solid multi-source reading experience with sync support
- [Like Manga](https://likemanga.ink/) rounds things out with a straightforward long-strip layout
</GuideBox>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<Download />} accent="amber">

### Downloading & Archives

</WeebSectionHeading>

<GuideBox title="Where to actually grab the files" icon={<HardDriveDownload />} tone="neutral">
- [Nyaa](https://nyaa.si/) covers most scanlated releases and raw scans in one place
- [Madokami](https://manga.madokami.al) runs an account gated archive with an enormous back catalog
- [Anna's Archive](https://annas-archive.gl/) and [Library Genesis+](https://libgen.li/) both mirror huge swaths of digital and scanned manga
- [Desuarchive](https://desuarchive.org/) and the Internet Archive's manga library act as long term backups when everything else goes dark
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Dedicated rippers worth following" icon={<Users />} tone="neutral">
A handful of individual uploaders consistently put out clean, well organized releases on Nyaa and 4chan archives. Names like 1r0n, LuCaZ, Shizu, and Stick show up constantly if you dig through upload histories.
</GuideBox>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<BadgeCheck />} accent="sky">

### Official & Legal

</WeebSectionHeading>

<WarnBox title="Availability shifts by region">
Not every publisher's storefront ships to every country, so double check before assuming a title is missing entirely.
</WarnBox>

<PillDeck>
  <WeebPill title="Manga Plus" href="https://mangaplus.shueisha.co.jp/" type="official" tagLabel="Free" />
  <WeebPill title="VIZ" href="https://www.viz.com/" type="official" />
  <WeebPill title="Yen Press" href="https://yenpress.com/" type="official" />
  <WeebPill title="Kodansha USA" href="https://kodansha.us/" type="official" />
  <WeebPill title="Seven Seas" href="https://sevenseasentertainment.com/" type="official" />
</PillDeck>

<GuideBox title="Chapter by chapter platforms" icon={<Layers />} tone="neutral">
These publish new chapters as they release rather than waiting for a full volume. Some are entirely free, others mix in a coin or subscription system:
- [Comikey](https://comikey.com/), [Manga UP!](https://global.manga-up.com/), and [Mangamo](https://www.mangamo.com/) all use a freemium coin model
- [KManga](https://kmanga.kodansha.com/) and [Coolmic](https://coolmic.me/) lean on subscriptions alongside free chapters
- [Shonen Jump](https://www.viz.com/sj-offer) bundles Weekly Shonen Jump chapters behind a subscription with a free tier
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Manhwa & manhua specific platforms" icon={<Globe2 />} tone="neutral">
Korean and Chinese webcomics mostly live on their own dedicated apps rather than general manga readers:
- [Webtoon](https://www.webtoons.com/en/) is the default entry point for most manhwa readers
- [Lezhin](https://www.lezhinus.com/en) and [Tappytoon](https://www.tappytoon.com/en/comics/discover) lean into mature and romance heavy titles
- [Tapas](https://tapas.io/), [Manta](https://manta.net/en), and [Toomics](https://toomics.com/en/) round out the rest with their own coin economies
</GuideBox>

- [Comic Book Reading Orders](https://comicbookreadingorders.com/) and [Continuity Guide](https://www.continuityguide.net/) cover a wider range of publishers beyond just Marvel
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="News, roundups & community takes" icon={<Heart />} tone="neutral">
- [13th Dimension](https://13thdimension.com/), [The Beat](https://www.comicsbeat.com/), and [The Comics Journal](https://www.tcj.com/) all cover industry news and long form commentary
- [Comic Book Roundup](https://comicbookroundup.com/) aggregates review scores across outlets
- [Comic Watch](https://comic-watch.com/) rounds things out with regular reviews and creator interviews
