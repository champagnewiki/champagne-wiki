## Tools & Utilities

</WeebSectionHeading>

<ZoneIntro accent="rose">
Downloaders, converters, image search engines, tracker stat sites, and a corner dedicated to archived otaku history. If it makes your setup smoother without being a full app, it lives here.
</ZoneIntro>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<Download />} accent="sakura">

### Downloaders

</WeebSectionHeading>

<GuideBox title="Anime & manga" icon={<HardDriveDownload />} tone="neutral">
[Anime Downloader NX](https://github.com/anidl/multi-downloader-nx) handles anime downloads from the command line. For manga, [Free Manga Downloader 2](https://github.com/dazedcat19/FMD2), [JAM](https://redsquirrel87.com/jam), and [gallery-dl](https://codeberg.org/mikf/gallery-dl/) cover most sources, while [HDoujin Downloader](https://doujindownloader.com/) specializes in doujin platforms.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Specific-case tools" icon={<Puzzle />} tone="neutral">
[BookWalker Downloader](https://gist.github.com/GolyBidoof/6c9cbff7644c7078520fe45e3bcf039f), [Clip Studio Reader Downloader](https://greasyfork.org/en/scripts/481576-clip-studio-reader-downloader), [Getjump](https://github.com/eggplants/getjump), and [Mangaplus Downloader NG](https://github.com/l0westbob/mloader) each target one specific platform's quirks.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Novels & DRM" icon={<ScrollText />} tone="neutral">
[WebtoEpub](https://github.com/dteviot/WebToEpub), [Epublifier](https://github.com/maoserr/epublifier), and [Lightnovel Crawler](https://github.com/lncrawl/lightnovel-crawler) convert web novels into readable ebook formats. For DRM removal, [KeyDive](https://github.com/hyugogirubato/KeyDive/), [N_m3u8DL-RE](https://github.com/nilaoda/N_m3u8DL-RE), [pywidevine](https://github.com/devine-dl/pywidevine), and [Unshackle](https://github.com/unshackle-dl/unshackle) are the current standard toolset.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Comics" icon={<BookOpen />} tone="neutral">
[Kapowarr](https://casvt.github.io/Kapowarr/) and [Mylar3](https://github.com/mylar3/mylar3) both automate comic downloading and library management, similar to how Sonarr works for TV.
</GuideBox>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<PlayCircle />} accent="sky">

### Anime Tools

</WeebSectionHeading>

<GuideBox title="Upscaling & metadata" icon={<Sparkles />} tone="neutral">
[AnimeJaNai](https://github.com/the-database/mpv-AnimeJaNai) upscales anime in real time through mpv, while [nativeres](https://github.com/Jaded-Encoding-Thaumaturgy/nativeres) helps identify a video's true native resolution. [Anicon for AniList](https://github.com/EnArvy/AniconforAnilist) generates custom icons from your AniList data, and [Manami](https://github.com/manami-project/manami) plus [Shoko Anime](https://shokoanime.com/) both handle local library organization.
</GuideBox>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<BookOpen />} accent="matcha">

### Manga Tools

</WeebSectionHeading>

<GuideBox title="Conversion & metadata" icon={<Layers />} tone="neutral">
[ACBR](https://github.com/binarynonsense/comic-book-reader), [CBconvert](https://github.com/gen2brain/cbconvert), [CbxConverter](https://tomeko.net/software/CbxConverter/), and [ComicbookArchiveToolbox](https://degoedel.github.io/ComicbookArchiveToolbox/) all handle format conversion and compression. For tagging, [ComicTagger](https://github.com/comictagger/comictagger) and [Manga Manager](https://github.com/MangaManagerORG/Manga-Manager) organize metadata across your library.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Covers & rendering" icon={<ImageIcon />} tone="neutral">
[DarkThumbs](https://github.com/fire-eggs/DarkThumbs) and [Icaros](https://www.videohelp.com/software/Icaros) fix thumbnail generation issues. [MangaJaNai](https://github.com/the-database/MangaJaNai) and its [Converter GUI](https://github.com/the-database/MangaJaNaiConverterGui) handle upscaling specifically for manga pages, while [XpdfReader](https://www.xpdfreader.com/index.html) and [Poppler](https://poppler.freedesktop.org/) cover PDF rendering needs.
</GuideBox>

### Server Software

</WeebSectionHeading>

<WeebSectionHeading icon={<Wrench />} accent="amber">

### Sync & Bridge Tools

</WeebSectionHeading>

<GuideBox title="Connecting servers to trackers" icon={<Compass />} tone="neutral">
[MAL-Sync](https://github.com/MALSync/MALSync) bridges nearly everything. For Jellyfin specifically: [Jellyfin-RPC](https://github.com/JustRadical/jellyfin-rpc), the [MAL Metadata Plugin](https://github.com/ryandash/jellyfin-plugin-myanimelist), and the [Shikimori plugin](https://github.com/te9c/jellyfin-plugin-shikimori). For Kavita and Komga: [komf](https://github.com/Snd-R/komf) and the [Komga Bulk Download Helper](https://github.com/darthmooguy/komga-bulk-download-helper). For Plex: [Plex to AniList Sync](https://github.com/RickDB/PlexAniSync) and [AniBridge](https://anibridge.eliasbenb.dev/). [Tailscale](https://tailscale.com/) is worth having regardless, for accessing your server securely from anywhere.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="A note on Mihon and forks" icon={<Puzzle />} tone="neutral">
Android apps built on the Mihon extension system (Aniyomi, Dantotsu, and their forks) can double as clients for Jellyfin, Komga, Kavita, and LANraragi once the right extension is loaded.
</GuideBox>

## Browser Extensions

</WeebSectionHeading>

<ZoneIntro accent="sakura">
Small quality-of-life additions for your browser, covering anime streaming, manga reading, tracker syncing, and general image or privacy utilities.
</ZoneIntro>

<GuideBox title="Anime" icon={<PlayCircle />} tone="neutral">
[animepahe Enhancer](https://github.com/abdullahkhfb/animepahe-enhancer), [Animepahe Plus](https://github.com/hwk929/Animepahe-plus), [Anime Skip Player](https://anime-skip.com/), [Aniskip](https://github.com/aniskip/aniskip-extension), [Crunchyroll With Better Seasons](https://github.com/Dragicafit/Crunchyroll-With-Better-Seasons), [Streaming Enhanced](https://github.com/Dreamlinerm/Netflix-Prime-Auto-Skip), and [Substital](https://substital.com/) all improve streaming site UX or add skip-intro functionality.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Manga" icon={<BookOpen />} tone="neutral">
[All Manga Reader](https://www.allmangasreader.com/) turns scattered manga sites into a unified reader interface. [Image Max URL](https://github.com/qsniyg/maxurl), [Manganato Enhancer](https://github.com/sn0w12/ManganatoEnhancer), and [Similar Manga](https://github.com/similar-manga/similar) each fix a specific site annoyance or add discovery features.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Trackers" icon={<Compass />} tone="neutral">
[MALSync](https://malsync.moe/) auto-tracks progress across most streaming and reading sites. [MAL Redesigned](https://hritikvaishnav.github.io/Project-Redesign/public/mal.html) refreshes MyAnimeList's dated UI, and [Web Scrobbler](https://github.com/web-scrobbler/web-scrobbler) extends scrobbling to Last.fm and similar services.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="Image" icon={<ImageIcon />} tone="neutral">
[Betterviewer](https://github.com/Ademking/Betterviewer) improves in-browser image viewing, while [Search by Image](https://github.com/dessant/search-by-image) adds reverse image search to your right-click menu across multiple engines at once.
</GuideBox>

<GuideBox title="General" icon={<Sparkles />} tone="neutral">
[Buster](https://github.com/dessant/buster) helps solve captchas faster, [Indie Wiki Buddy](https://getindie.wiki/) redirects fandom wiki links to ad-free alternatives, [Live Stream Downloader](https://webextension.org/listing/hls-downloader.html) grabs HLS streams directly, [NopeCHA](https://nopecha.com/) automates captcha solving, [Selection](https://github.com/emvaized/selecton-extension) adds a quick-action menu on text selection, [Translate Web Pages](https://github.com/FilipePS/Traduzir-paginas-web) handles inline translation, and [Violentmonkey](https://violentmonkey.github.io/) runs the userscripts referenced throughout this page.
</GuideBox>
