## Torrent Trackers

</GamingSectionHeading>

<WarnBox title="Uploader trust matters more than the site">
On general trackers like 1337x, safety depends entirely on **who uploaded the file**, not the site itself. Only download from names the community has vetted; everything else is a coin flip on malware.
</WarnBox>

<InfoDeck>
  <InfoCard title="1337x" href="https://1337x.to/" icon={<HardDrive />} tag="Torrent" tagStyle="rose">
    The most-used general torrent index for games, but only safe when filtered to known-trusted uploaders such as FitGirl, DODI, KaOsKrew, and johncena141.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="RuTracker" href="https://rutracker.org/forum/viewforum.php?f=899" icon={<HardDrive />} tag="Torrent" tagStyle="rose">
    A massive Russian-hosted tracker with an enormous games catalog, best used with a browser translator extension enabled.
  </InfoCard>
  <InfoCard title="Empress Releases" href="https://telegra.ph/empress-biography-07-15" icon={<HardDrive />} tag="Cracker" tagStyle="amber">
    The release hub for games cracked by Empress, one of the most respected crackers currently active for Denuvo-protected titles.
  </InfoCard>
</InfoDeck>

<GuideBox title="Trusted uploaders on 1337x" tone="neutral">
  FitGirl, DODI, KaOsKrew, johncena141 (Linux), Masquerade, r2tor, and Empress are the community-recognized safe names. Anything from CS.RIN.RU-linked accounts is also considered safe. Avoid unfamiliar uploaders entirely, especially ones with few or no prior releases.
</GuideBox>

## Cracking Tools & DLC Unlockers

</GamingSectionHeading>

<WarnBox title="Two very different categories, don't mix them up">
**DLC unlockers** (Koalageddon, SmokeAPI, CreamInstaller) only work on games you **already legitimately own** — they unlock paid DLC you haven't purchased separately, nothing more. **Steam emulators** (Goldberg, SSE, ALI213) bypass DRM entirely and are what actually lets pirated copies run. Most tools here require sign-up on CS.RIN.RU, and DLC unlocker tools additionally require the actual DLC files, usually linked in the game's CS.RIN.RU thread.
</WarnBox>
