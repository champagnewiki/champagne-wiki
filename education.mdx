<FieldNote title="Where to start with a new language" tone="special">
  Pick a route that matches how you actually like to learn.
  <ul>
    <li>Want structure? <strong>Language Transfer</strong> or your target language's dedicated course (DW German, TalkToMeInKorean) gives you a syllabus</li>
    <li>Want immersion? <strong>Language Reactor</strong> or Japanese's <strong>Immersion Kit</strong> teach through native media instead of textbooks</li>
    <li>Want a conversation partner? <strong>Tandem</strong> or <strong>Conversation Exchange</strong> connect you with native speakers directly</li>
  </ul>
</FieldNote>
