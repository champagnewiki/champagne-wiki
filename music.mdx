## Finding Your Next Favourite Record

</MusicSectionHeading>

"I don't have anything to listen to." Not on our watch. These are the charts, maps, and tracking engines worth losing an afternoon to. 

<ToolDeck columns={2}>
  <ToolCard title="RateYourMusic (RYM)" href="https://rateyourmusic.com/" badge="Database" badgeStyle="wine" icon={<Disc />} starred>
    The ultimate community-driven music database. Incredible for finding highly specific, user-rated custom charts across every genre imaginable. The absolute best place on the internet to discover deep cuts and critically acclaimed masterpieces.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Every Noise At Once" href="https://everynoise.com" badge="Genre Map" badgeStyle="brass" icon={<Globe />} starred>
    A sprawling, algorithmic scatter-plot of over 6,000 musical genres based on Spotify tracking data. Click any obscure genre to hear a sample, find its core artists, and fall down a rabbit hole.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Last.fm" href="https://www.last.fm/home" badge="Tracker" badgeStyle="emerald" icon={<ListMusic />}>
    Tracks (or "scrobbles") your listening habits across all your devices and streaming platforms to build a massive, visual profile of your musical taste and recommend new artists based on your unique data.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Chosic" href="https://www.chosic.com/" badge="Discovery" badgeStyle="rose" icon={<Music2 />}>
    Discover new music based on your favorite bands. Generates spot-on similar artist lists and helps you break out of algorithmic echo chambers by analyzing the sonic profile of the tracks you love.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Kworb" href="https://kworb.net/" badge="Charts" badgeStyle="neutral" icon={<Zap />}>
    Deep streaming statistics—track top songs, artists, and album movement by country across Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube to see what the world is actually listening to in real-time.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Music-Map" href="https://www.music-map.com" badge="Visual Map" badgeStyle="brass" icon={<Map />}>
    A floating visual artist relationship map. Type in one musical act you love and watch the screen populate with a floating constellation of similar artists. The closer two names are, the more likely you are to enjoy them.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Every Noise World Browser" href="https://everynoise.com/worldbrowser.cgi" badge="Global" badgeStyle="wine" icon={<Search />}>
    See exactly what Spotify editors around the world are programming in their local Browse sections. Filter music by country, region, and city to hear the local underground sound.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Spotify Charts" href="https://charts.spotify.com/home" badge="Official" badgeStyle="emerald" icon={<ListMusic />}>
    The official real-time streaming charts, breaking down the most streamed songs, viral hits, and top artists globally and by specific region.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Skiley" href="https://skiley.net" badge="Web App" badgeStyle="rose" icon={<Sparkles />}>
    A powerful webapp that integrates directly with your Spotify account to manage playlists, discover brand new tracks, and analyze your library with advanced sorting algorithms.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Dubolt" href="https://dubolt.com/" badge="Generator" badgeStyle="brass" icon={<Music2 />}>
    Discover new music strictly through the lens of your old favorites. Plug in the artists you love and let it generate a tailored discovery playlist for you.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Gnoosic" href="https://www.gnoosic.com/" badge="AI Engine" badgeStyle="neutral" icon={<Zap />}>
    A wonderfully simple, quiz-based recommendation engine. Rate a few bands you like (I like, I don't like, I don't know), and it feeds you a highly curated shortlist of new ones.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Forgotify" href="http://forgotify.com/player.cfm" badge="Obscure" badgeStyle="wine" icon={<Disc />}>
    Plays only tracks that have never once been played on Spotify. Give a totally forgotten, zero-playcount song its first spin in the world.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Playlist Miner" href="http://playlistminer.playlistmachinery.com/index.html" badge="Aggregator" badgeStyle="emerald" icon={<Library />}>
    Aggregates the top tracks from the most popular public playlists on Spotify based on your exact search criteria (e.g., search "workout" to find the tracks that appear most frequently in fitness playlists).
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="4chan Music Wiki" href="https://4chanmusic.miraheze.org/wiki/4chanmusic_Wiki" badge="Community" badgeStyle="rose" icon={<BookOpen />}>
    A community-driven wiki dedicated to deep-cut music discussion, legendary essentials charts, and discovery, heavily inspired by the infamous /mu/ board.
  </ToolCard>
</ToolDeck>

<GuideBox title="Scrobbling & Listening Analytics" tone="brass">
  If you want deeper insights into what you listen to, pair Last.fm with tools like **<a href="https://openscrobbler.com/">OpenScrobbler</a>** to manually log vinyl/CD listens, or **<a href="https://github.com/kawaiiDango/pano-scrobbler">Pano Scrobbler</a>** for seamless mobile tracking. **<a href="https://listenbrainz.org/">ListenBrainz</a>** and **<a href="https://github.com/krateng/maloja">Maloja</a>** are fantastic open-source and self-hosted alternatives to Last.fm.
</GuideBox>

## Firehawk52 — Hi-Res Tokens & Accounts

</MusicSectionHeading>

To utilize tools like OrpheusDL, QobuzDownloaderX, or Streamrip for premium FLAC downloads, you traditionally need an active, paid subscription to those services. **Firehawk52** bridges that gap by providing community-sourced authentication tokens.

<GuideBox title="The Firehawk52 Hub" tone="wine">
  The project has officially migrated its primary hub to <a href="https://firehawk52.com"><strong>firehawk52.com</strong></a>. The old Rentry page is being phased out, so update your bookmarks.
  
  * **What they provide:** A running supply of free ARLs (Deezer), tokens (Qobuz), and account access for Tidal to plug into your ripping software.
  * **Qobuz 403 Error Fix:** Qobuz recently changed its authentication system. If your ripper throws an "invalid username/password" error, Firehawk52 has a dedicated guide on their site to fix the `app_id` and secret handling.

<MusicSectionHeading kicker="Curation" icon={<ListMusic />} accent="sky">

## Playlist Transfer & Management

</MusicSectionHeading>

Whether you are migrating away from Spotify, backing up a massive library, or trying to fix a broken shuffle algorithm, these tools give you total control over your curated playlists.

<ToolDeck columns={2}>
  <ToolCard title="TuneMyMusic" href="https://www.tunemymusic.com/" badge="Transfer" badgeStyle="emerald" icon={<Zap />} starred>
    The standard for transferring massive playlists between almost any music service (Spotify, Apple Music, Tidal, YouTube). Fast, reliable, and handles heavy libraries with ease.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Soundiiz" href="https://soundiiz.com/" badge="Sync Manager" badgeStyle="emerald" icon={<Globe />} starred>
    An advanced playlist manager. Goes beyond simple transfers by allowing you to synchronize playlists across different platforms, merge lists, and automatically clean up broken tracks.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Spotify Dedup" href="https://spotify-dedup.com/" badge="Cleanup" badgeStyle="wine" icon={<Sparkles />}>
    Spotify's interface frequently allows duplicate tracks to bloat your playlists. This open-source tool safely scans your library and removes duplicates with a single click.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Trikatuka" href="https://trikatuka.aknakn.eu/#/" badge="True Shuffle" badgeStyle="brass" icon={<Sliders />} starred>
    Fixes Spotify's infamously broken shuffle algorithm. This tool completely randomizes your track order and clones it into a new, genuinely shuffled playlist.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Exportify" href="https://exportify.app/" badge="Backup to CSV" badgeStyle="neutral" icon={<Download />}>
    Instantly back up any of your Spotify playlists by exporting them directly into a clean CSV file. Essential for preserving your library data outside of the ecosystem.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Spotlistr" href="https://www.spotlistr.com/" badge="Text Converter" badgeStyle="rose" icon={<Search />}>
    An incredibly resourceful tool that converts plain text lists, Reddit threads, YouTube links, or Last.fm scrobbles directly into Spotify playlists in seconds.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Playlisty" href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/playlisty-for-apple-music/id1459275972" badge="iOS App" badgeStyle="rose" icon={<Smartphone />}>
    The absolute gold-standard iOS app for Apple Music users. Seamlessly imports playlists from Spotify, YouTube, and Setlist.fm directly into your Apple library.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Discover Quickly" href="https://discoverquickly.com/" badge="Fast Builder" badgeStyle="emerald" icon={<Headphones />}>
    Built by Spotify employees, this visual interface lets you simply hover your mouse over tracks and album art to instantly hear a preview, making playlist building exponentially faster.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Smarter Playlists" href="http://smarterplaylists.playlistmachinery.com/" badge="Automation" badgeStyle="brass" icon={<Map />}>
    A powerful node-based graphical interface for building complex Spotify playlists. Set automated rules to filter tracks by BPM, acousticness, or combine multiple lists dynamically.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Spotify Backup" href="https://github.com/caseychu/spotify-backup" badge="CLI Script" badgeStyle="neutral" icon={<Monitor />}>
    A lightweight, open-source Python script for developers and data-hoarders to quietly export your entire Spotify library and playlist data via the command line.
  </ToolCard>
  <ToolCard title="Playlistor" href="https://playlistor.io/" badge="Apple Music" badgeStyle="neutral" icon={<Zap />}>
    A streamlined, no-frills web tool specifically designed for fast, accurate conversions directly between Apple Music and Spotify.
  </ToolCard>
</ToolDeck>
