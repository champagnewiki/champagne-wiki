## Everyday Mobility

</FitnessSectionHeader>

Regular mobility, stretching, and flexibility exercises are critical for maintaining optimal physical health and function—especially if you spend hours at a desk. These routines improve range of motion, prevent injuries, fix posture, and reduce muscular tension.
